About LifeLink & Donor Eligibility

LifeLink is dedicated to reducing preventable mortality by providing instant, transparent, and secure matching between emergency patients and voluntary blood donors.

Our Core Healthcare Philosophy

Every two seconds, someone in the healthcare system requires blood. During sudden trauma, major surgery, acute anemia, or pediatric chemotherapy, the difference between life and death is measured in minutes. Traditional blood banks often face localized inventory imbalances, and family members are left making desperate calls.

LifeLink transforms emergency blood logistics by utilizing a decentralized, real-time registry of voluntary donors who keep their availability updated. Through automated ABO/Rh compatibility triage, we ensure the right donor is notified for the right patient without delay.
