Clinical Health Screening Questionnaire

Please answer all 10 questions truthfully. This preliminary self-check safeguards both you and the blood recipient. Certified hospital blood banks conduct full laboratory cross-matching before collection.

1. In the last 7 days, have you had fever, cold, flu, or felt unwell?
2. Have you taken antibiotics in the last 7 days?
3. Have you had a tattoo, body piercing or acupuncture in the last 12 months?
4. Have you had major surgery in the last 6 months?
5. Have you had malaria in the last 3 months?
6. Are you pregnant, or have you given birth / been breastfeeding in the last 12 months?
7. Do you have high or low blood pressure that is not under control?
8. Have you ever tested positive for HIV, Hepatitis B, Hepatitis C or syphilis?
9. Do you have heart disease, cancer, kidney or liver disease, epilepsy, or a bleeding disorder?
10. Do you have diabetes that is treated with insulin?
