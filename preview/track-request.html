Mandatory Clinical & Safety Advisory

A donor’s response in this portal indicates their voluntary personal willingness to donate. It DOES NOT represent verified biological cross-match, laboratory clearance, or guaranteed blood availability at the medical facility.

For life-threatening or immediate critical requirements: Please contact an authorized hospital blood bank, certified blood center, or call emergency medical services immediately.

Emergency Helpline: 911 / 108 / 112
