About LifeLink

Who can donate, and who can receive

LifeLink connects patients with eligible volunteer donors nearby. Every donor answers a health screening when they register; donors who do not pass are hidden from search until they are eligible again.

Basic eligibility checklist

  • Age 18 to 65 years and body weight at least 45 kg
  • Hemoglobin at least 12.5 g/dL
  • At least 90 days since your last donation (120 days for women)
  • No fever, cold or antibiotics in the last 7 days
  • No tattoo or piercing in the last 12 months, no major surgery in the last 6 months, no malaria in the last 3 months
  • Not pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Never tested positive for HIV, Hepatitis B/C or syphilis; no heart disease, cancer, epilepsy, bleeding disorder or insulin-treated diabetes

This is a basic self-check. The blood bank always does a full medical check before you donate.
